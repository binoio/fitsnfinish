FITS n' Finish 0.4.5

  • Fixed: crash on launch. 0.4.4 aborted immediately on open when installed anywhere but the machine that built it. The Metal shader was being located through an absolute build-directory path instead of the app bundle; the app now finds its resources relative to wherever the .app lives (~/Applications, /Applications, or elsewhere) and falls back to the CPU pipeline rather than crashing if they are ever missing.
